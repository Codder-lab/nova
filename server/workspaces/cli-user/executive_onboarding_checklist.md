# Executive Onboarding Checklist

## Week 1: Foundation & Relationship Building
- [ ] Meet with CEO and direct reports to establish rapport and expectations
- [ ] Tour company facilities and understand physical/workspace layout
- [ ] Review company mission, values, and strategic objectives
- [ ] Set up essential accounts and access (email, Slack, CRM, etc.)
- [ ] Schedule one-on-one meetings with key stakeholders
- [ ] Review organizational chart and reporting structure
- [ ] Understand budget authority and approval processes

## Week 2: Deep Dive & Strategy Alignment
- [ ] Review last 3 months of financial performance and key metrics
- [ ] Analyze current market position and competitive landscape
- [ ] Meet with department heads to understand operational challenges
- [ ] Review ongoing projects and their status
- [ ] Identify immediate wins and quick impact opportunities
- [ ] Establish 30-60-90 day goals and success metrics
- [ ] Set up regular check-in cadence with CEO

## Week 3: Process & Systems Assessment
- [ ] Review existing processes and workflows
- [ ] Assess technology stack and tooling effectiveness
- [ ] Evaluate team performance and structure
- [ ] Identify gaps in skills, resources, or capabilities
- [ ] Review risk management and compliance frameworks
- [ ] Understand customer journey and key touchpoints
- [ ] Establish communication protocols and reporting lines

## Week 4: Integration & Planning
- [ ] Synthesize findings from weeks 1-3
- [ ] Draft initial 90-day action plan
- [ ] Present findings and plan to leadership team
- [ ] Identify immediate priorities and resource needs
- [ ] Set up development meetings for key initiatives
- [ ] Establish feedback mechanisms with team members
- [ ] Begin relationship building across broader organization

## Month 2: Execution & Optimization
- [ ] Launch priority initiatives identified in week 4
- [ ] Conduct team assessments and one-on-ones
- [ ] Review progress against 30-day goals
- [ ] Adjust strategies based on early feedback
- [ ] Build cross-functional partnerships
- [ ] Identify and address any cultural integration issues
- [ ] Refine 60-day goals and metrics

## Month 3: Strategic Review & Forward Planning
- [ ] Present 90-day comprehensive review to leadership
- [ ] Evaluate what's working and what needs adjustment
- [ ] Develop strategic plan for next 90 days
- [ ] Identify long-term opportunities and challenges
- [ ] Establish performance review framework
- [ ] Build executive presence and influence
- [ ] solidify role as strategic partner to CEO/Board

## Ongoing (First 6-12 Months)
- [ ] Monthly business review meetings
- [ ] Quarterly strategic alignment check-ins
- [ ] Continuous relationship building across organization
- [ ] Monitor industry trends and competitive shifts
- [ ] Regular check-ins with mentor or coach
- [ ] Personal development and learning goals
- [ ] Work-life balance and boundary setting
